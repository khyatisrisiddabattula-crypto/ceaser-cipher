How Caesar Cipher Works

        

          The Caesar Cipher shifts every letter forward by a fixed number of positions in the alphabet.
          A shift of 3 turns A → D, B → E, … and wraps X → A.
          Decryption reverses the shift. Numbers, spaces, and punctuation are left untouched.
